# Design System

Selected style: wuhan-jade-ivory, a project-specific bright anime / warm Chinese cultural editorial system adapted from heritage-warm-brown. Match the supplied approved 江城有灯 poster's established character identity, teal brush-lettered logo and painterly detail, but use lighter ivory negative space and more jade/river-blue; avoid an all-sepia cast.
Canvas: exactly2048x1152, landscape16:9, one full-page finished bitmap. All titles, diagrams, labels, logo and page numbers generated inside the image. No outer mockup or border.
Palette: ivory#F6EEDD paper, dark teal#174F52 text, jade#709C8C, river-blue#84B4B6, warm brass#C99D4F, natural brick accents. Fine paper texture, no dirty/grunge overlay.
Typography: refined clear simplified-Chinese Song-inspired headings with restrained hand-lettered character; brush calligraphy only for game logo. Header72–90px, claim38–44px, body/diagram labels34–40px, footer26–28px. Latin tool names and URL use a clean precise readable type. Body never becomes microtext. Left align explanatory text, group labels directly with the matching visual.
Margins: essential text at least90px from left/right,70px top/bottom. Consistent small 江城有灯 brand at upper left on body pages only; cover uses big title instead. One unobtrusive footer "AIGC宣传插画 · 游戏介绍" at bottom left and the supplied page number bottom right. Generate these once only.
Art: high-quality warm hand-painted anime game illustration, beautiful precise architecture and adult character anatomy, established 阿遥 short chestnut bob, cream cardigan with green ribbon details, black lace-trimmed top, dark green skirt, black tights, beige floral satchel. Granny grey hair/round glasses/mauve cardigan; Cai Yi tied dark hair/orange shirt/green apron. Keep their faces, ages and clothing consistent; don't create baby-like proportions.
Strong content-specific focal image, about45–60% of body page. Mix narrative panorama, physical objects, illustrated workflow and paper album layouts across deck. No repeating generic corporate card grid.
No fabricated awards, official logos, sponsor/partner badges, team names, team size, fundraising, charts or statistics beyond exact supplied text. No photorealistic actor faces, neon AI orb, robots, hospital motifs, unrelated template words, fake screenshot label, Steam logo, QR code, 60FPS/zero-bug claims, or random background lettering. No title punctuation change except an intentional line break; preserve exact supplied Chinese.
